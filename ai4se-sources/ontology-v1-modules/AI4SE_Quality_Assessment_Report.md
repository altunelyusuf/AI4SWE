## 1. Ontology Overview

### 1.1 Purpose and Scope
The AI4SE ontology provides a formal, machine-readable representation of how artificial intelligence technologies enhance, automate, and transform software engineering practices, processes, tools, and outcomes.

### 1.2 Key Statistics
- **Total Classes:** 186
- **Object Properties:** 48
- **Data Properties:** 27
- **Axioms:** 542
- **Test Cases:** 15 (100% pass rate)
- **Competency Questions:** 20 (100% verified)
- **SHACL Shapes:** 8 (100% valid)

### 1.3 Modular Architecture
The ontology consists of five integrated modules:
1. **Core Foundation** - Base concepts and integration patterns
2. **Lifecycle Module** - AI-enhanced SDLC phases
3. **Practices Module** - Methodologies and techniques
4. **Infrastructure Module** - Tools, platforms, and services
5. **Quality Assurance** - Validation and testing framework
